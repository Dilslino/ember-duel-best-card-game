EMBER DUEL

A card duel of flame, tide, gale & stone

You face the Cinder Warden — 30 life each, 3 ember to start.
Each turn: draw a card, gain 2 ember, play what you can afford.
Play two cards of the same element in one turn to trigger a SURGE.
Reduce the Warden to 0 life before yours burns out.

▲ FLAME +3 dmg ● TIDE heal 4 ◆ GALE draw 1 ■ STONE +6 shield

Strike cards deal damage. Guard cards raise your shield — shield absorbs damage first.

Kindling cards give ember or draw cards. Ember is your mana: every card costs ember to play.

Your deck has 24 cards (12 designs × 2). When it runs out, the discard pile is reshuffled.

Keyboard: 1–7 play a card · E end turn · M mute · R rematch.

An original game — art, names and sounds made for this duel. No network, no account.

🏆

VICTORY!

The Cinder Warden crumbles to ash.

🥀

DEFEAT

Your ember fades. The Warden keeps the forge.
